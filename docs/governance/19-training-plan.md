# 19. Training Plan

Cascade model for national scale: a central team trains state master trainers; master trainers train district trainers; district trainers brief officers.

| Level | Audience | Duration | Content | Material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Master trainer | 2-3 per state (SJPU, police training college) | 1 day | Everything below plus policies, incident reporting, how to collect feedback | Docs 05, 18, SOP, DPIA summary |
| T2 District trainer | 1-2 per district | Half day | Officer briefing, hands-on, failure drills, lost-phone procedure | Guide 18, UAT scripts as exercises |
| T3 Officer briefing | Field officers, SJPU, women and child desk | 15-30 minutes | Quick card, 3 practice scenarios, do's and don'ts | Quick card, demo phone |

## Officer briefing outline (15 minutes)

1. What the app is and is not (2 min).
2. Live demo: lost child, offline triage (4 min).
3. Practice in pairs: officer and subject swap (6 min).
4. Privacy rules and ending a session (2 min).
5. What to do when it fails (1 min).

## Sensitisation (part of T1 and T2)

- Basics of Deaf culture and ISL etiquette, ideally delivered by a Deaf trainer (ISLRTC or a Deaf association).
- Trauma-informed first contact with children, delivered by a child-welfare professional.
- Legal points: JJ Act sections 10 and 74, POCSO section 26, BNSS sections 38, 47, 48, 53, 58, 173 and 183(6), BSA section 125, Constitution Article 20(3); why the app never records statements; rights shown are not proof of understanding.
- Reading the record: evidence grades, answer trails, and why app and officer readings are not the person's answers.
- Second screen: pairing, the check number, Break given, the hand-back screen, and keeping a child in sight.

## Measures

- Officers who complete UAT-02 unaided after briefing: target 90%.
- Feedback form after first real use; reviewed monthly by the master trainer.
