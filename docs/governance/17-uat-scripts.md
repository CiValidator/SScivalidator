# 17. User Acceptance Test Scripts

Run by department officers on department phones with the release candidate. One tester acts as officer, one as subject. Record Pass / Fail / Notes per step. A script passes when every step passes. The [user guide](18-user-guide.md) describes each screen.

| Script | Steps | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UAT-01 First run | Install; open; read the three screens; pick Hindi for the app; set a screen lock if asked | The notice says "aid, not interpreter"; the app refuses to start sessions until a screen lock exists |
| UAT-02 Lost child | Airplane mode on. Choose Young child and Hindi; start; show "I am a police officer", "Are you hurt?", "Do you want water?"; subject answers with Yes, No and a picture | Each question appears at once with the character, caption, picture and speech; officer sees each answer with its time; nothing needs a network |
| UAT-03 Sensitive gate | Try to open the Sensitive group | Needs a deliberate confirmation; no sensitive question on the first screen |
| UAT-04 Deaf subject | Choose Signs; show "Are you hurt?"; subject answers No; officer uses They gestured for a gesture the subject makes and shows it back | Adult character, no sound; show-back asks "Is this it?" and only Yes records the meaning |
| UAT-05 Own words | In the type box, type "Where is Ravi's home?"; leave the default toggles; tap Send | The stage shows the caption with each word lit in turn: the asking sign for "Where", "Ravi" spelled letter by letter with the letter shown, the home sign with the home picture; nothing reaches the subject before Send |
| UAT-06 Phrase | Phrases, "Do you want ...?", water; show it | The full sentence appears with the picture; the character signs it in parts |
| UAT-07 Stop and don't know | Subject taps I don't know, then Stop | Both logged; Stop opens the pause layer for the subject, which only I'm ready ends, and shows the officer a banner with guidance; the log records the pause length |
| UAT-08 Drawing | Subject draws a house and finishes | Drawing appears in the session log |
| UAT-09 Language finder | Choose Other language; start; subject taps their language | Their greeting plays; back on the officer panel the session is in that language |
| UAT-10 Back to officer | On the stage, try Home, Back and recents; then tap the lock | The subject cannot leave the stage by Home, Back or recents; the lock returns to the panel; with Screen lock to leave the stage on, the fingerprint or PIN is asked as well |
| UAT-11 Interruptions | Take a phone call mid-session; leave the app for six minutes; reopen | Session intact; app asks for unlock |
| UAT-12 Interpreter | Call an interpreter from the panel | Messaging app opens at the department contact; no session details are sent |
| UAT-13 Export and end | End session; export; share the report; give the password separately; finish | Report opens only with the password; session gone from the phone |
| UAT-14 Privacy | Try a screenshot on the stage and the panel; open recents | Screenshot blocked; recents thumbnail blank |
| UAT-15 Auto-delete | Leave an unended session past the auto-delete period; reopen | Session deleted |
| UAT-16 Old and small phones | Repeat UAT-02 and UAT-04 on the oldest supported phone and on a 5-inch phone | Same results; answer cards whole; character smooth |
| UAT-17 Large text | Set the phone's font size to largest; repeat UAT-02 | Every screen readable; no text cut off |
| UAT-18 Second screen | Two phones (one on API 29 with a fingerprint enrolled, one on API 34 or later) and one tablet. (1) Pair them in system Bluetooth settings. (2) On the stage device cancel the first unlock prompt and tap Use as stage screen; the officer types the code, both screens show the same check number, the stage taps Match; then type a wrong code three times on a new pairing. (3) Show each item type. (4) Answer with each answer type. (5) Take the stage out of range for under 120 s. (6) Out of range for over 120 s. (7) Tap Stop while Reconnecting. (8) Unpin the stage with the system gesture. (9) Press the stage's power button. (10) Leave the officer screen idle for 6 minutes while Live. (11) Leave the app on the officer phone, then wait 5 minutes. (12) End the session. (13) On API 29 without a fingerprint, try Use as stage screen. (14) On the debug build, `adb shell run-as in.signsetu ls -R` on the stage after the session. (15) Repeat with a child-profile session | (2) Live only after Match; three wrong codes close the link. (3) Every item shows on the stage. (4) Each answer is logged on the officer phone only, marked from second screen. (5) The link resumes with no code; unacknowledged answers arrive marked late. (6) The stage shows the hand-back screen and stays pinned. (7) The break screen shows, and the Stop reaches the officer after the resume; Show stays blocked until Break given. (8) The officer is alerted within about 2 s. (9) The stage reappears, not the keyguard. (10) No relock. (11) The stage shows Hold, then Ended. (12) The stage stays pinned on the hand-back screen until its credential. (13) Refused. (14) No new files other than the settings store. (15) The support person stays within sight of the stage throughout |
| UAT-19 Auto | (a) Allow Auto in Settings; start an Auto session and approve through to closing, overriding at every step with chips and the composer. (b) The subject taps Stop; tap Resume; the subject taps Stop again. (c) Kill the process in Auto, then unlock. (d) Leave the hold screen untouched for 2 minutes | (a) Only welfare items are proposed; each needs an approval; the report shows the mode and who chose each item. (b) Auto pauses; Resume shows "Can we carry on?"; the second Stop ends Auto. (c) The session reopens in Manual with no proposal pending, and the export marks the last suggestion "not acted on". (d) The app locks |
| UAT-20 Signed answer | On a 64-bit phone, choose Signs; show "Do you want water?"; tap Read signs; the subject signs two signs fast, then slowly; tap Done; replay at ¼×; enter a word per sign; tap Ask them to choose; the subject picks one, then None of these | The stage shows one dot per sign and never a guess; "A little slower, please" appears only after a failed read; the log shows the app's reading and the officer's reading as readings; each set of choices is logged before it appears; the pick is the subject's answer and None of these is Unclear |
| UAT-21 Account | On More, tap Tell me; the subject signs for about a minute; Done; replay later in the session; end the session | No timer or cue on the stage; replay works until the session ends; after ending, nothing of the signing remains and the export holds no landmarks |
| UAT-22 Rights | Set the role to Accused or in custody; open the rights groups; show one item; then mark "may be under 18" and open them again | The JJ notice is shown and logged; adult arrest rights give way to the child's rights; each item shows the interpreter line to the officer and on the stage; the transcript and export say "shown, not proof of understanding"; no free-recall or sensitive group is offered |
| UAT-23 Opening and pause | Start a session; the subject moves through the four ground rules and the practice round with Next; in a second session take the phone back during the ground rules | Each step is logged as shown; the second session logs "Officer skipped the rest of the ground rules" |
| UAT-24 Handover | Prepare a question; tip the phone over its top edge toward the subject and hold; on the stage press one volume key, then hold both for 1.5 s; turn on TalkBack and press a volume key | The question shows and the stage turns to face the subject; single presses do nothing; both keys return to the officer (with the credential if set); with TalkBack the keys change the volume |
| UAT-25 Sun mode | Outdoors in direct sun, show a question; move into shade | The stage turns black, white and yellow after about 3 s and back after about 10 s in shade |
| UAT-26 Crash on the stage | Show a question; force-stop the app from Settings; unlock the phone and open the app at once | The full fingerprint or PIN is asked, not skipped |

**Sign-off:** department nominee signs the completed sheet; failures become defects with severity.
