# Content Contribution, Review and Approval Policy: Sign Setu

Everything a subject sees is content: triage questions, captions, translations, picture cards, ISL clips and interface text on the subject panel. This policy covers how it is created, reviewed, approved, changed and archived (GIGW content lifecycle requirements, adapted for an app).

## 1. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Content owner | Maintains the content set; final approver of releases of content |
| Contributor | Proposes new or changed content |
| Language reviewer | Fluent speaker; checks each translation for meaning and tone |
| ISL reviewer | Qualified ISL user or interpreter (for example ISLRTC-trained or from a Deaf association); checks every clip |
| Child-welfare reviewer | Counsellor, CWC member or child psychologist; checks wording and pictures for safety and suitability |

## 2. Standards

- Wording follows the content style in [UX](../03-ux.md) §7: short, gentle, one idea, non-leading, safety first.
- Pictures: simple illustrations, no photographs of real people, no violence, weapons, injuries shown graphically, or frightening imagery; culturally appropriate for India; diverse in skin tone, dress and region.
- ISL clips: one signer style across the set; plain background; signer's face clearly visible; no more than 6 seconds per question.
- Every asset has a recorded source and licence (see [copyright and licensing policy](copyright-licensing-policy.md)).

## 3. Process

1. Contributor submits the change with the reason.
2. Language reviewer checks each language version.
3. ISL reviewer checks any new or changed clip.
4. Child-welfare reviewer checks subject-facing wording and pictures.
5. Content owner approves; the approval is recorded with names and date.
6. Content ships in the next app release (content-only changes need no re-audit; see [document 11](../11-devsecops-release.md) §7).

No step may be skipped for triage questions. Machine translation may be used as a draft but never released without review.

## 4. Review cycle

- Full content set reviewed before the pilot, after the pilot using feedback, and yearly after that.
- Any complaint about a picture, translation or sign triggers review of that item within 10 working days; harmful content is removed in the next release or sooner through an emergency release.

## 5. Archival

Retired content versions are kept in the repository history with their approval records, so the content shown in any past release can be reconstructed.
