- Need review before use: LGPL, MPL, EPL, CC-BY (for data or models).
- Not allowed in the app without legal approval: GPL, AGPL, any non-commercial or no-derivatives licence, components with no licence.
- Every component appears in the SBOM with its licence. The app's Settings, About screen lists open-source licences.

## 6. Avatar characters

Avatar characters are original designs made by the project team in VRoid Studio and Blender. Rights in the parts the team creates (textures, hair, expressions, clips) belong to the department; every VRoid preset item used is recorded in the licensing register with its terms. No commercial cartoon character, or a design confusingly similar to one, may be used (see [avatar design](../../05-avatar-and-gestures.md) §4). The legal adviser checks the final design before animation starts.
