# Data Retention and Disposal Policy: Sign Setu

**Owner:** Privacy lead. **Review:** yearly, and when data flows change.

## 1. Rule

Personal data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, then deleted (DPDP Act section 8(7) and Rule 8). The official record of a case lives in the department's case systems, not in this app.

## 2. Retention schedule

| Data | Location | Retention | Disposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Session log, answers, notes, drawings | Officer's phone, encrypted | Until the session is ended, or the auto-delete period (default 24 hours, configurable between 4 and 72 hours by the department), whichever comes first | Deleted from the database; database compacted |
| Export file | Wherever the officer sends it | Governed by the recipient system's rules | Officer deletes local copies after handover |
| Camera images | Phone memory | Not retained; discarded frame by frame | Automatic |
| Landmarks sent for recognition | Transit; partner service | Not retained by app; partner must not retain | Partner confirms in writing |
| Voice audio | Phone memory; transit; Bhashini | Not retained by app; Bhashini terms [confirm] | Buffer cleared after upload |
| Gateway access logs | India-region cloud | 180 days rolling (CERT-In Directions 2022) | Automatic expiry |
| Audit reports, incident records | Department records | As per department record retention rules | Per department rules |
| Settings | Officer's phone | Until uninstall or "clear all data" | Automatic |

## 3. Officer duties

- End the session when the conversation is over.
- Export only what the case record needs, to the official destination, and delete local export copies after handover.
- Never copy session content into chat apps, personal notes or photos.

## 4. Device disposal

Before a phone is reassigned or disposed of: use "Clear all data", uninstall the app, and factory reset the phone through department procedure.

## 5. Legal hold

If a court or competent authority requires that data be preserved, the officer exports the session to the official record before the auto-delete period ends and records the instruction. The app does not provide a hold that suspends deletion.
