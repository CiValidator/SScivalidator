# Accessibility Statement: Sign Setu

*Draft. Publish with the store listing after the accessibility review.*

<Department name> is committed to making Sign Setu usable by everyone, as the app exists to help people with disabilities communicate.

## Standards

The app aims to meet:

- Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and Apps (GIGW) 3.0
- IS 17802 (Parts 1 and 2), Accessibility for ICT products and services, as required under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA, which both of the above reference

## Features

- Indian Sign Language for questions, with captions and pictures
- Answers by pictures, signs or drawing; no reading or speaking required from the person being helped
- Large touch targets and high contrast
- Text follows the phone's font size setting
- Officer controls labelled for screen readers (TalkBack)
- No flashing content, no time limits on the subject's side
- Hindi, English and <regional languages>

## Known limitations

- The sign language avatar for free text may be harder to understand than a human signer; preset questions use recorded human signers.
- The app does not recognise signs. When an officer reads a gesture, the person confirms its meaning on the screen.
