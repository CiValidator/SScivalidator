# 18. Officer User Guide and Quick Card

## Quick card (print on one side, A6)

```
SIGN SETU - QUICK CARD
It helps you talk. It is NOT an interpreter. It never records a statement.

1. Calm place. Sit at their level. Unlock, choose WHO you are helping
   and THEIR language, tap START SESSION.
2. Turn the phone to face them. The character greets them.
3. Ask one question at a time: Safety first, then Health and Needs.
   Wait. Never ask what happened first.
4. They answer with YES, NO, I DON'T KNOW, STOP, a picture or a drawing.
5. STOP means pause: check they are comfortable, offer water or rest.
6. To take the phone back, tap the small lock at the top right of their
   screen and use your fingerprint or PIN.
7. Done: menu > END SESSION. Export the report only if the case needs it,
   and give the password separately from the file.
8. Phone lost? Tell your supervisor now.
Child in distress: Childline 1098 or 112.
```

## Before first use

- Set a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). Sign Setu does not store anything without one.
- The first three screens explain the app, set its language, and show how to turn on app pinning ("Ask for PIN before unpinning").
- In **Settings > Interpreter call**, enter the interpreter contact your department gives you (WhatsApp or Sandes). Without it the call button stays hidden.

## Guide

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Open the app and unlock with your fingerprint or phone PIN. An **Unfinished session** card offers **Continue** or **End it**. |
| Who you are helping | Tap a face: **Young child**, **Signs** (sign language users, adult character, no sound), **Other language** (they choose their language first), or **Not sure**. Then choose **Their language** and tap **Start session**. Allow the camera when asked: it shows them their own hands and records nothing. |
| The stage | The phone faces them. The character gestures or signs, a speech bubble shows the words in their language, the picture is held up, and their hands appear in the mirror below with the answer cards. **Yes**, **No**, **I don't know** and **Stop** are always there. The app stays pinned so they cannot open other apps. |
| Replay | The play button repeats the question; **½×** repeats it slowly. |
| Take the phone back | Tap the small lock at the top right of the stage, then your fingerprint or PIN. |
| Their answer | Every answer gets the same small nod and "okay". You see it as **Latest answer**; the full list is in **Session log**. |
| Preset question | Tap a group (Safety, Health, Needs, Identity, Family, Location), tap a question, check the bar at the bottom, tap **Show to them**. The hand and speaker marks on a question show that a gesture and a recording exist. |
| Sensitive questions | The **Sensitive** group is locked until you confirm. Ask only when the person is calm and safe, and never first. |
| Phrases | **Phrases** group: pick a sentence ("Do you want ...?"), then the picture it is about. The character signs the sentence in parts. |
| Pictures | **Pictures** opens the board: categories, search and your **Favourites** (tap the star). Pick one or more and tap **Use**. |
| Your own question | **Type**: write a short question and add pictures if you like. They see your words and the pictures; the character does not sign typed words. |
| They made a gesture | **They gestured**: tap where the hand went and what it did, then the likely meaning. They see it with "Is this it?"; only their **Yes** records the meaning. |
| Drawing | **Draw**: they draw with a finger and tap done; the drawing is kept with the session. |
| Find their language | **Language**: they see greetings in many languages and tap theirs. |
| They sit opposite you | Menu, **They sit opposite me**: the stage turns upside down for them. |
| Camera mirror | Menu, **Camera mirror** turns it off for this person, for example if seeing themselves upsets a child. |
| Interpreter | The phone button opens a call to the interpreter contact in your messaging app. No session details are sent. |
| Notes | **Session log**, **Add a note**. Write only what the case needs. |
| End | Menu, **End session**: **Export report** (a password-protected PDF; the password is shown once), then **Share report** and **Finish and delete session**. Ending deletes everything on the phone and cannot be undone. |
| Settings | Interpreter call, camera mirror, large captions, slower gestures, how long unfinished sessions are kept, delete all data, app language. |

Sessions you do not end are deleted automatically after the time set in Settings. The app locks itself after five minutes in the background.

## Troubleshooting

| You see | Meaning | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set a screen lock first | The phone has no screen lock | Open security settings, set a PIN, come back |
| Earlier sessions were deleted | The screen lock was removed or changed, so Android deleted the key | Nothing; new sessions work as normal |
| This phone looks rooted | The phone's security may be weakened | Tell your supervisor; avoid storing sessions on it |
| Update required | This copy of the app is over 180 days old | Install the current version from your department; you can still finish an open session |
| Content check failed | The questions or pictures do not match the approved set | Install the current version from your department |
| Some pictures, sounds or gestures failed a check | A few items were left out | The rest works; report it to your department |
| A hand picture instead of the camera | Camera permission off, camera in use, or mirror turned off | Use the answer cards; allow the camera in Android settings if you want the mirror |
| Android's "Screen pinned" notice | The phone confirms pinning | Tap **Got it**; taps on the app are ignored while the notice shows |
