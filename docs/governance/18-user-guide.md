# 18. Officer User Guide and Quick Card

## Quick card (print on one side, A6)

```
SIGN SETU - QUICK CARD
It helps you talk. It is NOT an interpreter. It never records a statement.
Statements need a qualified interpreter and the video recording.

1. Calm place. Sit at their level. Unlock, choose WHO you are helping,
   their ROLE if known, and THEIR language, tap START SESSION.
2. Turn the phone to face them. The character greets them and shows
   the ground rules; they tap NEXT.
3. Ask one question at a time: Safety first, then Health and Needs.
   Wait. Never ask what happened first.
4. They answer with YES, NO, I DON'T KNOW, I DON'T UNDERSTAND,
   THAT'S WRONG, BREAK, STOP, a picture, a drawing, writing or signs.
5. STOP or BREAK means pause: check they are comfortable, offer water
   or rest. Wait for them to tap I'M READY.
   Signs read by the app are a guess: let THEM choose the meaning.
6. To take the phone back, tap the small lock at the top right of their screen.
7. Done: menu > END SESSION. Export the report only if the case needs it,
   and give the password separately from the file.
8. Phone lost? Tell your supervisor now.
Child in distress: Childline 1098 or 112.
```

## Before first use

- Set a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). Sign Setu does not store anything without one.
- The first three screens explain the app, set its language, and show how to turn on app pinning ("Ask for PIN before unpinning").
- In **Settings > Interpreter call**, enter the interpreter contact your department gives you (WhatsApp or Sandes). Without it the call button stays hidden.

## Guide

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Open the app; if you have just unlocked the phone it opens straight away, otherwise use your fingerprint or phone PIN. An **Unfinished session** card offers **Continue** or **End it**. |
| Role | On Home or in the change sheet: **Not known yet** (default), **Victim or witness**, or **Accused or in custody**. Choosing Accused shows you the Juvenile Justice notice; mark **The person may be under 18** if that may be so. |
| Who you are helping | Tap a face: **Young child**, **Signs** (sign language users, adult character, no sound), **Other language** (they choose their language first), or **Not sure**. Then choose **Their language** and tap **Start session**. Allow the camera when asked: it shows them their own hands and records nothing. |
| The stage | The phone faces them. The character gestures or signs, a speech bubble shows the words in their language, the picture is held up, and their hands appear in the mirror below with the answer cards. **Yes**, **No**, **I don't know** and **Stop** are always there. The app stays pinned so they cannot open other apps. |
| Replay | The play button repeats the question; they choose the signing speed (½×, ¾×, 1×). Tapping a word replays it alone, slowly. |
| Opening | The session starts with your introduction, four ground rules and a practice round ("Show me what you did this morning"). They tap **Next**. If you take the phone back early the log says you skipped the rest. |
| Show without tapping | With something ready, tip the top of the phone over toward them and hold for a moment, or press **Volume Up**. The screen turns to face them. Turn either off in **Settings > Stage**. |
| Take the phone back | Tap the small lock at the top right of the stage, or hold both volume keys for 1.5 seconds. If your department turned on **Screen lock to leave the stage**, your fingerprint or PIN is asked as well. |
| Officer strip | The band at the edge facing you shows the last question and answer upside down. Long-press it to hide it for the session. You also feel the answer: one long buzz for Yes, two for No, three short for Stop and the other "not sure" answers, a tick for a picture. |
| Their answer | Every answer gets the same small nod and "okay". Under each answer you see its grade (**Said**, **Confirmed**, **Officer's reading**, **App's reading**, **Said no**, **Unclear**), how long it took, and any change. An amber outline means look again, not that they are lying. |
| Say it back | Under an answer, **Say it back**: edit your reading and show it. They pick the meaning among pictures; only their pick makes it **Confirmed**. |
| Several questions at once | Long-press quick questions, or tap **Safety basics**: up to five go on one handover. Sensitive questions are never queued. |
| Preset question | On **More**, tap a group (Safety, Health, Needs, Identity, Family, Location, Talking), tap a suggestion card, check **Ready to show**, tap the send button. The hand and speaker marks on a card show that a gesture and a recording exist. Shown questions and their answers build up as a conversation above. |
| Sensitive questions | The **Sensitive** group is locked until you confirm. Ask only when the person is calm and safe, and never first. |
| Phrases | **Phrases** group: pick a sentence ("Do you want ...?"), then the picture it is about. The character signs the sentence in parts. |
| Pictures | **Pictures** opens the board: categories, search and your **Favourites** (tap the star). Pick one or more and tap **Use**. |
| Rights | For an accused, the rights groups appear. Show each one; a card reminds you that rights must also be explained through a qualified interpreter, and the record says shown, not understood. |
| Auto | If your department allowed it, switch to **Auto**: a card suggests the next welfare question; tap to show it, skip it, or choose something else. Stop pauses Auto; a second Stop ends it. |
| They sign an answer | Tap **Read signs** (or they tap the hand on their screen). They sign; tap **Done**. You see the app's reading, marked not checked. Replay it at ¼× or ½×, type a word per sign, then **Ask them to choose**: they see two or three pictures per sign and "None of these". Only their tap counts. |
| They want to tell you what happened | On **More**, **Tell me**: up to 3 minutes of signing, with no timer on their screen. You or an interpreter can replay it later in the session. Nothing is kept after the session ends. Never treat it as a statement. |
| Your own question | Type in the composer at the bottom and tap send. Coloured dots on the suggestions show what the character can do with a word. The character signs, gestures or spells it word by word and holds up pictures; the toggles above the composer choose Signs, Gestures, Spell and Pictures. **+** then **Add pictures** attaches answer pictures. |
| Their language differs from yours | Type your question, tap **Assistant** (if your department installed it). Check **Which means**: it is the translation read back in English. If it is right, tap send; if not, close the card and your words are shown as typed. |
| They write an answer | On the stage they tap **Write**, type, and tap **Done**. Their words appear in your conversation as a typed answer. A young child does not get the button. |
| They made a gesture | **+**, then **They gestured**: tap where the hand went and what it did, then the likely meaning. They see it with "Is this it?"; only their **Yes** records the meaning. |
| Drawing | **Draw**: they draw with a finger and tap done; the drawing is kept with the session. |
| Find their language | **Language**: they see greetings in many languages and tap theirs. |
| They sit opposite you | Menu, **They sit opposite me**: the stage turns upside down for them. |
| Camera mirror | Menu, **Camera mirror** turns it off for this person, for example if seeing themselves upsets a child. |
| Bright sun | The stage turns black, white and yellow by itself; menu, **Sun mode** forces it on. |
| Second screen: set up | Pair the officer phone and the stage device once in **Bluetooth settings**. On a new stage device, cancel the first unlock prompt, then tap **Use as stage screen**. On your phone, menu, **Second screen**, pick the device and type the 6 digits it shows. Your phone shows a check number; the stage shows the same number and the person holding it taps **Match** only if they agree. |
| Second screen: in use | A bar shows the device, its battery and the last answer. If the link drops, tap **Reconnect**, or **Use this phone** to bring the stage back. If they tap Stop on the second screen, give a break and tap **Break given** before showing anything else. |
| Second screen: end | When you end the session the stage stays on a hand-back screen, pinned, until the stage device's own fingerprint or PIN is given. Collect the device yourself. |
| Interpreter | The phone button opens a call to the interpreter contact in your messaging app. No session details are sent. |
| Notes | **Session log**, **Add a note**. Write only what the case needs. |
| End | Menu, **End session**: **Export report** (a password-protected PDF; the password is shown once), then **Share report** and **Finish and delete session**. Ending deletes everything on the phone and cannot be undone. |
| Settings | Interpreter call, camera mirror, large captions, slower gestures, screen lock to leave the stage, tilt to show, volume keys, allow automatic conversation, the assistant model, how long unfinished sessions are kept, delete all data, app language. |

Sessions you do not end are deleted automatically after the time set in Settings. The app locks itself after five minutes in the background.

## Troubleshooting

| You see | Meaning | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set a screen lock first | The phone has no screen lock | Open security settings, set a PIN, come back |
| Earlier sessions were deleted | The screen lock was removed or changed, so Android deleted the key | Nothing; new sessions work as normal |
| This phone looks rooted | The phone's security may be weakened | Tell your supervisor; avoid storing sessions on it |
| Update required | This copy of the app is over 180 days old | Install the current version from your department; you can still finish an open session |
| Content check failed | The questions or pictures do not match the approved set | Install the current version from your department |
| Some pictures, sounds or gestures failed a check | A few items were left out | The rest works; report it to your department |
| A hand picture instead of the camera | Camera permission off, camera in use, or mirror turned off | Use the answer cards; allow the camera in Android settings if you want the mirror |
| Android's "Screen pinned" notice | The phone confirms pinning | Tap **Got it**; taps on the app are ignored while the notice shows |
| "A little slower, please" on their screen | The app could not read fast or hidden hands | Let them sign again; never ask them to sign one sign at a time up front |
| No Read signs button | The phone is 32-bit, or the camera is off | Use pictures, Yes/No, drawing or writing |
| "That does not match" when pairing | The code was mistyped, or it is the wrong device | Look at the other screen again; after three misses start again |
| "Different versions of Sign Setu" | The two devices run different builds or content | Update both from your department |
| "The second screen was unpinned" | Someone left the app on the stage device | Check on the person and the device at once |
