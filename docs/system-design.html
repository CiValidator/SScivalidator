System design · Release 1

Sign Setu

An offline Android app that helps a police officer communicate with a lost, rescued or kidnapped person who cannot communicate in words: often a child who is deaf, non-verbal, traumatised, or speaks another language. Officer and subject share the officer's phone. The app is a communication aid, not an interpreter, and never records statements.

What it does

Officer and subject take turns with one phone. The officer prepares or types something and taps Send; the phone faces the subject, who sees it on the stage and answers; the officer takes the phone back with one tap and reads the answer in the conversation.
