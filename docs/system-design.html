1Scope and design principles

1.1Purpose

Sign Setu is an Android application that lets a police officer communicate with a lost, rescued or kidnapped person who cannot communicate in words, often a child who is deaf, non-verbal, traumatised or speaks another language. Officer and subject share the officer's phone. The application is a communication aid: it does not interpret, and it never records statements.
